How long does the Third Eye Opening experience take?+

Approximately 2.5–3 hours, depending on the number of participants.

What is included in the experience?+

The experience includes Trimala Ghanta Purification, Astha Murthi Ghanta Yoga and the Third Eye Opening session.

How many people can join?+

The duration may vary depending on the number of participants. Contact Bali Mantra for group arrangements.

What should I wear?+

We recommend comfortable and respectful clothing suitable for a spiritual experience.

Do I need previous yoga experience?+

No previous yoga experience is required unless otherwise specified for a particular experience.

Where is Bali Mantra located?+

Bali, Indonesia. The exact meeting location will be provided during booking.
