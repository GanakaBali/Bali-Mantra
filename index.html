Good to know

Questions &
answers.

How long does the Third Eye Opening experience take?+

Approximately 2.5–3 hours, depending on the number of participants.

How long is the yoga session?+

Approximately 30–45 minutes, depending on the number of participants.

What is included in the experience?+

The signature journey begins with Astha Murthi Ghanta Yoga, continues with Trimala Ghanta Purification with three sacred waters, and concludes with a guided Third Eye Opening practice. Each stage is designed to offer space for reflection and inner awareness.

What do the three sacred waters represent?+

In this ritual, red, black and white holy waters are traditionally associated with the physical body, subtle body, and mind and spirit. The ritual is a spiritual practice, and each guest’s experience is personal.

How many people can join?+

The duration may vary depending on the number of participants. Contact Bali Mantra for group arrangements.

What should I wear?+

We recommend comfortable and respectful clothing suitable for a spiritual experience.

Do I need previous yoga experience?+

No previous yoga experience is required unless otherwise specified for a particular experience.

Where is Bali Mantra located?+

Bali, Indonesia. The exact meeting location will be provided during booking.
